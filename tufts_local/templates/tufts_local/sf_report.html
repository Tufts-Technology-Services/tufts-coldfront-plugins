Storage Allocations vs. Starfish Top-level Directories Report

This report shows the relationship between storage allocations in Coldfront and top-level directories in Starfish. It identifies allocations that are missing a Starfish volume path attribute (sf_vol_path), allocations that have a Starfish volume path attribute but do not exist in Starfish (likely deleted or archived), and top-level directories in Starfish that do not have a corresponding allocation in Coldfront. An Allocation may need to be created in Coldfront for these directories if they are still in use.
